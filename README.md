# Rutz's PF2e Forge

Make gear durability visible and part of play.

- **Durability** — weapons and armor get GM Core material Hardness, HP and BT; HP bars and broken tags on sheets.
- **Broken & destroyed** — broken armor gets its AC penalty as an effect, broken weapons an attack penalty (or rules as written), destroyed weapons can't attack.
- **Repair** — players roll their own Repair check; results applied by the rules.
- **Plus** — wear during combat with a post-combat upkeep window (party HP, conditions, Focus Points, XP), and crafting projects that progress day by day (works with Rutz's PF2e Days).

## Lite & Plus
This is the free edition; the **Plus** features above come with a Patreon membership (same module, so worlds keep their data). During the open beta every feature is free.
